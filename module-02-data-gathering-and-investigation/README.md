# Module 2: Getting Started with Data Gathering and Investigation

**Course:** Cisco Networking Academy — Data Analytics Essentials

**Module objective:** Perform initial data gathering and investigation using a spreadsheet.

## Overview

This module focuses on the early stages of working with data: finding relevant datasets, understanding what they contain, and investigating whether they're suitable for analysis.

I'll be exploring the tools commonly used in data analytics, including Excel, SQL, and Tableau, along with Kaggle as a source of publicly available datasets. The module also covers the fundamentals of datasets, metadata, observations, variables, and different types of data.

These notes bring together the key concepts, examples, practical activities, and revision points from the course. The goal is to build a solid understanding of how data is gathered and investigated before moving on to deeper analysis.
